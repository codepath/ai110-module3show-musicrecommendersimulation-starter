# Genre Glossary

Short, plain-language descriptions of the genres in the MoodMatch catalog. These
are the reference documents the chat assistant retrieves from when a user asks
what a genre *is*, rather than asking for a recommendation.

## Lofi
Lofi (short for "low-fidelity") is relaxed, downtempo music built around mellow
beats, soft instrumentation, and a warm, slightly hazy sound. It is popular as
background music for studying, focusing, and unwinding. In the catalog it pairs
with chill and focused moods and low energy.

## Pop
Pop is melodic, catchy, and produced for broad appeal, usually with bright vocals
and a strong beat. It tends toward higher energy and happy or upbeat moods.

## Rock
Rock is guitar-driven and often high-energy, ranging from anthemic to intense. In
the catalog it carries an amplified, heavy sound and pairs with intense moods.

## Metal
Metal is the loudest, most aggressive rock offshoot: heavily distorted guitars,
fast tempos, and very high energy. It sits at the extreme end of the intensity
range.

## EDM
EDM (electronic dance music) is produced electronically for dancing, built on
synthesized sounds and a steady, danceable beat. It ranges from bright to dark and
usually sits at high energy.

## Hip hop
Hip hop centers rhythmic vocals (rap) over beats, with strong danceability and
groove. Energy varies from laid-back to energetic.

## R&B
R&B (rhythm and blues) blends smooth, soulful vocals with groove-oriented
production. It leans toward romantic and relaxed moods at moderate energy.

## Jazz
Jazz is improvisational and expressive, built on swing, syncopation, and live
instrumentation. In the catalog it is relaxed and acoustic.

## Classical
Classical is orchestral or instrumental art music, often acoustic and dynamic. In
the catalog it appears in low-energy, melancholic form (e.g. solo piano).

## Folk
Folk is acoustic, story-driven music built on organic instruments like guitar and
voice. It tends toward gentle, dreamy, low-energy moods.

## Ambient
Ambient is atmospheric, texture-first music with little or no beat, meant to
create a mood or space. It is very low energy and highly acoustic/organic.

## Reggae
Reggae is a rhythmic Caribbean style with an offbeat groove and an uplifting feel,
usually at moderate energy.

## Synthwave
Synthwave is retro-futuristic electronic music inspired by 1980s soundtracks, with
lush synthesizers and a moody, nocturnal feel.

## Indie pop
Indie pop is melodic pop with a more independent, less mainstream production style.
It shares pop's brightness at slightly lower polish.

## Country
Country is rooted in acoustic guitar and storytelling, often nostalgic in mood at
moderate energy.
